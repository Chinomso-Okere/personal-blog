Motion, and what each one is for

Arrival. Hero lines rise out of a mask once, 1.1 s, then stay still.
Feedback. Underlines draw, arrows nudge 4 px, buttons press to 98%, index rows get an accent hairline sweep.
Context. Images ease in 3% on hover. Dark mode cross-fades over 0.45 s from a switch that slides.
Confirmation. Copy turns accent and reads Copied for 1.8 s.
In the build. Reading progress follows scroll, the contents list tracks the section, and pages cross-fade between routes.
Always. Nothing loops, and reduced-motion turns it all off.
