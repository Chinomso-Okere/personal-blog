## Document Structure and Hierarchy

Typography establishes a natural progression of ideas across sections. When body paragraphs follow a heading, text flows comfortably with consistent line height and tone. You can emphasize key points using **strong font weight** or highlight terms with *delicate emphasis*.

Links integrated into the flow of reading, such as [an external reference](https://example.com), receive a subtle underline in the secondary accent ink with standard offset.

### Secondary Section Details

Technical write-ups frequently require inline references. Variable names like `max_iterations`, function parameters such as `threshold=0.66`, or file identifiers like `config.json` sit comfortably alongside running text with subtle contrast.

> A well-crafted pull quote draws immediate focus, standing out with an accent border while maintaining consistent serif proportion.
